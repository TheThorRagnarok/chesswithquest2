03 How a collab works

Three periods. No overtime.

  1. 1st 20:00

    Brief

    We agree on the goal, the audience and one clear message. You get a short plan with formats and dates before anything is filmed.

  2. 2nd 20:00

    Shoot

    Filmed in real situations: practice, game day, travel. Your product shows up where it is actually used, not in a staged corner.

  3. 3rd 20:00

    Publish and report

    Posts go live on the agreed dates. Afterwards you get the numbers straight from the platform: reach, views, saves and shares.
